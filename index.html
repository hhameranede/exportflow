05GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Is ExportFlow a store or a courier?

Neither. ExportFlow is a tracking interface connected to carrier integrations listed in its catalog.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to check a parcel in this version.

Why is my shipment not updating?

Carriers may take time to publish a new scan. ExportFlow can only show information the carrier makes available.
