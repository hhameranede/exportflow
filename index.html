05GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Is ExportFlow a store or a courier?

Neither. ExportFlow is a parcel-tracking experience that brings deliveries from different stores and carriers together in one place.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Start with a tracking number and get a clear view without creating an account.

Why does a shipment sometimes pause?

A carrier may need time to register its next scan. We show the latest confirmed update and keep the timeline easy to follow.
