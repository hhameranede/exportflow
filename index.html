GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Is ExportFlow a store or a courier?

Neither. ExportFlow is a parcel-tracking experience designed to help you follow deliveries from different stores and shipping carriers in one place.

Do I need to create an account?

The first version is designed to let you check a parcel without creating an account. Saved parcels and alerts can be added later.

Why doesn't my tracking number show an update yet?

A carrier may need time to register the first scan. This prototype uses sample data only; live carrier lookups will be connected in a later development step.
