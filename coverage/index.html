How to read this directory

Direct means a selectable carrier record has an automatic dedicated adapter.

Fallback entries are separate and may depend on postal number format and the enabled UPU provider.

Link-only means a tracking page exists in the catalog, but ExportFlow does not query it automatically.

No catalog entry does not mean no carrier operates in that country. It means the installed catalog has no listed route for it today.

Catalog source: Universal Parcel Scraper, pinned revision e64a4667. Country/territory list: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2. Carrier presence is not an official partnership and is not a live-tracking success guarantee.
