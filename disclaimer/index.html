Important disclaimer.

Please understand what ExportFlow can — and cannot — confirm about a shipment.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Independent tracking interface

ExportFlow is an independent parcel-tracking project. It is not a courier, postal operator, shipping company, freight forwarder, customs broker, marketplace or government service.

Carrier data and availability

Shipment status, scan history, locations and estimated delivery dates are provided by third-party carrier systems or configured tracking sources. Data may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable or changed by its source. ExportFlow cannot independently verify every event and does not guarantee successful tracking for every carrier, country or parcel.

Carrier names and trademarks

Carrier names, logos and trademarks are the property of their respective owners. They are used only where needed to identify a carrier or tracking source. Their presence does not imply authorization, partnership, sponsorship or endorsement.

Country and carrier directory

Directory entries describe the carrier records and integration modes available in the installed catalog. A listed country or carrier does not guarantee that a particular parcel can be tracked, that the carrier operates in every region of that country, or that ExportFlow has a commercial relationship with that carrier.

Official shipment decisions

Do not use ExportFlow as the sole source for legal, customs, insurance, delivery-dispute or proof-of-delivery decisions. Contact the carrier, seller or relevant authority for official confirmation.
