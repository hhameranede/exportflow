YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy.

This notice explains what information may be processed when you use ExportFlow, why it is needed and how to contact us about it.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Important: ExportFlow is an independent project. This policy describes the current website and tracking implementation, not practices that have not been implemented. It is not a legal certification or a representation that ExportFlow has completed every regulatory filing that may apply.

1. Information you provide

  • Tracking details: a parcel tracking number, and optionally a carrier identifier and delivery postcode if you submit them for a lookup.
  • Contact details: information you choose to include when emailing or calling us. Please do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.
  • Language preference: the selected language may be stored in your browser's local storage so the site can remember your preference.

2. How tracking information is used

When you submit a tracking request, the website sends the tracking number and any optional carrier, postcode and language fields to the ExportFlow tracking endpoint. The endpoint validates the request, applies rate limits and sends the necessary shipment lookup information to the relevant carrier integration or fallback provider. The response is used to display carrier-returned status and scan events.

Do not submit a tracking number unless you are authorized to use it. A tracking number and postcode may reveal shipment information, so treat them as private.

3. Technical and security information

The tracking endpoint uses the connecting IP address supplied by the hosting platform to derive a temporary hashed key for rate limiting. The application code does not intentionally retain raw IP addresses or parcel numbers in that rate-limit map. The hosting provider and network services may separately process technical logs, request metadata and security information under their own terms and retention practices.

Our website loads certain technical assets from third parties, including web fonts and the Leaflet library. Shipment maps may request map tiles from OpenStreetMap's tile service when a map is displayed. Those providers may receive technical connection information when your browser requests their resources. Review their own privacy notices for details.

4. Cookies and analytics

The current website stores the language preference in browser local storage. We do not currently intentionally deploy advertising cookies or third-party analytics tags in the site code reviewed for this policy. This may change; if it does, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before the relevant technology is introduced.

See our Cookie Policy for more information.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Tracking data is shared with the carrier integration or fallback provider needed to attempt the lookup. Infrastructure and asset providers may process technical data to deliver and secure the website. We do not claim that all processing occurs in Algeria; international service providers may process information in other countries.

6. Retention

The current tracking interface does not provide user accounts or a shipment-history feature. We do not define a fixed retention period for provider-side logs in this notice because those periods depend on the relevant infrastructure and carrier providers. Contact us if you have a question about information you submitted directly to the project.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit a tracking number or optional postcode. You can clear the saved language preference through your browser's site-data settings. You may contact us to ask about information you have provided, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify the request and may be unable to fulfil requests where a legal exception applies.

8. Algeria and other applicable laws

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and to assess applicable data-protection requirements, including Algerian Law No. 18-07 as amended and supplemented by Law No. 25-11 of 24 July 2025. This policy is not a statement that ExportFlow has obtained regulatory approval or completed any notification or authorization procedure. Applicable rights and obligations depend on the processing activity and the law that applies.

9. Children

ExportFlow is a general parcel-tracking tool and is not designed to collect information from children. Do not submit another person's personal information unless you are authorized to do so.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the service or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date. For privacy questions, email hhameranede@gmail.com.
