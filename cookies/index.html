Cookie Policy.

A plain-language explanation of cookies and similar technologies used by the current ExportFlow website.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Cookies

The current ExportFlow website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies in its first-party application code. Our hosting, security or third-party providers may use technical mechanisms under their own services and policies.

Local storage

ExportFlow stores your selected language in your browser's local storage under the key exportflow-language. This helps remember your language between visits. It is not a cookie and can be removed through your browser's site-data settings.

Third-party resources

The website loads web fonts and the Leaflet mapping library from external providers. If a tracking map is shown, your browser may request map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. Their own privacy and cookie policies apply.

Your choices

You can manage cookies and clear local storage through your browser settings. Blocking some third-party resources may affect fonts or map display, but should not prevent the basic tracking form from loading.

Updates

If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential storage technologies, we will update this page and add any consent mechanism required by applicable law before using them.
