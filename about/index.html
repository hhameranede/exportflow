ABOUT THE PROJECT

Every parcel.
One clear view.

ExportFlow is an independent parcel-tracking project designed to make shipment information easier to follow by bringing carrier-reported updates into one readable timeline.

Independent project· exportflow.me

Our mission

Shipping information is often spread across different carrier websites, formats and tracking experiences. ExportFlow aims to make that information easier to access and understand from one place.

How ExportFlow works

You provide a tracking number and, where needed, a carrier or delivery postcode. ExportFlow checks its carrier integration catalog and requests available tracking information. When a carrier returns scan events, we organize those events into a clearer timeline.

Tracking availability and the detail returned depend on the carrier, shipment, route and the information made available by the carrier. We do not create missing scans, invent delivery events or guarantee that every shipment can be tracked.

Independent service — not a courier

ExportFlow is not a postal operator, courier, freight forwarder, online store or customs authority. Carrier names and catalog entries are shown to help identify possible tracking sources; they do not mean that ExportFlow is officially partnered with, sponsored by or endorsed by those carriers.

Project status and ownership

ExportFlow is currently presented as an independent technology project. This website does not represent that the project is a registered company, a licensed carrier, or an ISO-certified organization. Any future legal registration, certifications or formal partnerships will be described only after they have been completed and can be verified.
